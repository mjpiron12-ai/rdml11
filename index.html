Core doctrine
01

Trunk before branches

Identity, consequence, and purpose come before polish. Every system needs a center of gravity before it needs styling.

02

Coherence over decoration

Remove trend residue, fake-premium styling, glow-heavy clutter, and surfaces with no structural job.

03

The Lyra review

Every output passes an internal critic. Is this coherent? Would a stranger understand this in 3 seconds? Comfort is not a principle.

04

Intelligent texture

Texture should imply interdependence, breathable order, and calm complexity — never ornamental confusion.

05

Consequences, not counts

"3 overdue callbacks" tells you nothing. "$4,200 stalled" tells you everything. Show what matters, not what exists.

06

Reality precedes presentation

Distinguish observed from inferred, estimated from confirmed. Trust is created when reality and representation remain aligned.
